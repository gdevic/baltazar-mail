Where: the chip on the left shows the scope, the open folder and its subfolders (or Starred). Matches in other folders are listed below a divider. Click the chip, or press Backspace in the empty box, to switch to all mail; Shift+Enter searches all mail at once.

Only capital AND, OR, NOT are operators. Words are matched in the text, subject and addresses. Searches that combine words with AND or NOT check up to the 500 newest matches of each word; the status line says when there were more.
